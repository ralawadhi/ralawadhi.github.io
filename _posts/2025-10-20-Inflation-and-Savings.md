Suppose you have saving $P_n$ where $n\in \mathbb{N}$. Suppose the years inflation rate is around $I\in [0, 1]\in\mathbb{R}$  means . You savings after $n$ amount of years is then
$$ P_n = P_1 e^{-In}. $$
Lets say I want to know how many years it would take for my savings to halve. Then,
$$\frac{1}{2}P_1 = P_1 e^{-In}.$$
Therefore the number of years needed for our savings to halve at an [[inflation]] rate of $I$ is
$$n = \frac{\ln 2}{I}.$$
So if $I=0.02$ then $n \approx 34.7$ years.
